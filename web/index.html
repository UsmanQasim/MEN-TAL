✳ BEFORE YOU STEP IN

A few fair questions.

Is MEN-TAL free?

Yes. The web app is free to use. An optional contribution link may be added later, but it is not required and no payment is currently connected.

Do I need to make an account?

No. Your web app check-ins and journal are saved in this browser on your device. Don't use a shared device for private notes.

Can MEN-TAL replace therapy or emergency support?

No. MEN-TAL is a small wellbeing companion. It isn't monitored and can't provide diagnosis, treatment, or crisis care. If you may be in immediate danger, call your local emergency number now.

Can I use it on my phone?

Yes. Open the web app in your phone's browser. If your browser offers it, choose “Add to Home Screen” or “Install app” for quick access. Your entries remain in that browser on that device.
