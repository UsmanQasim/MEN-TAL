A CLEAR AND GENTLE BOUNDARY

Terms & safety.

Use MEN-TAL as a small wellbeing companion—not as a substitute for qualified care, a diagnosis, or emergency help.

Effective date: October 21, 2026 · Applies to the MEN-TAL website, web app, and mobile app.

Operator details are not yet configured. The project owner must add a legal name and contact details to this page before public release. This plain-language summary is not legal advice and should be checked for the jurisdictions where the service will be offered.

What MEN-TAL provides

MEN-TAL offers general self-care prompts, a mood check-in, breathing and grounding exercises, an on-device journal, and links to external information and support directories. The tools are optional; you can stop an exercise or leave any activity at any time.

What MEN-TAL does not provide

MEN-TAL is not a healthcare provider, medical device, therapist, diagnostic service, or crisis line. Its content is educational and supportive, not medical advice or a treatment plan. It does not monitor your safety, read your journal, or alert anyone if you are in danger. Do not delay professional care or emergency services to use an app.

If you may hurt yourself or someone else, or are in immediate danger, call your local emergency number or go to the nearest emergency department now. If possible, tell someone you trust and ask them to stay with you. Find emotional support by country at Find A Helpline.

Your choices and device

You are responsible for deciding whether the app is suitable for you and for protecting access to your device. Web entries are stored in your browser; mobile app entries use platform secure storage. Anyone who can unlock or access your device may be able to access information stored on it. Read the Privacy Policy for details and deletion options.

External services

Links to helpline directories, information providers, country lookup, or a future optional creator page lead to services MEN-TAL does not operate. Their content, availability, privacy, and terms are controlled by their operators. A country lookup is approximate and not a substitute for selecting the correct local emergency service.

Using the service responsibly

Do not misuse the service, interfere with its operation, or rely on it to provide a response in an emergency. MEN-TAL may change or discontinue features to maintain the service and safety of its users. Nothing here limits rights that cannot legally be limited in your location.
