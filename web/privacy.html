YOUR SPACE SHOULD FEEL LIKE YOURS

Privacy, in plain language.

Your feelings are personal. MEN-TAL is designed to keep your check-ins and journal on your device, not in an account or our server.

Effective date: October 21, 2026 · Applies to the MEN-TAL website, web app, and Android/iOS app.

Before public launch: The person or organization operating MEN-TAL has not provided a legal name, business address, or privacy contact email. Who operates MEN-TAL?

MEN-TAL is an independent wellbeing project. Operator/legal name, postal address, and privacy contact: to be supplied by the project owner before public release. Do not treat this draft as complete until a real contact is published.

What the apps store on your device

Web app

Your daily mood check-in, daily care-task progress, and journal entry are saved in this browser's local storage on the device you are using. MEN-TAL does not send these entries to its server and has no user account, advertising, or analytics system. Anyone who can use your browser profile or device may be able to access them. Browser storage can be removed by clearing this site's data in your browser settings; clearing the journal inside the app removes the saved journal entry.

Android and iOS apps

The mobile app stores check-ins, daily-task progress, and journal notes locally using Expo SecureStore backed by Android Keystore or iOS Keychain. MEN-TAL does not operate an account or app-data server. Secure storage helps protect saved data but is not an absolute guarantee against access. On iOS, Keychain values may remain after the app is uninstalled. Protect your device and avoid recording information you would not want someone with access to it to see.

Optional contribution page

No donation or payment link is currently configured. If the owner later adds one, following it takes you to the named external payment provider. That provider processes information under its own privacy policy and terms; MEN-TAL does not receive payment details.

When information leaves your device

Core check-ins, journal writing, daily tasks, and breathing/grounding exercises work without sending that content to MEN-TAL. External requests happen only when you choose an online feature or link:

  • Find my country: If you tap the country lookup button, your device requests an approximate country from ipwho.is. Your internet-facing IP address is visible to that service, which returns a country name and country code. MEN-TAL does not save the lookup result. IP handling and retention by ipwho.is are governed by its own policies and terms. You can skip detection and choose a country in the helpline directory instead.
  • Helplines and information: Opening Find A Helpline, WHO information, or another external site shares the information normally sent when your browser visits that site. Its operator controls what happens next.
  • Hosting and delivery: The website's hosting provider and your network/browser may process ordinary connection data, such as IP address, request time, and requested page, to deliver and protect the site. MEN-TAL does not currently have a named host or access to its log-retention configuration. Check the hosting provider's policy and configure disclosures to match the host you actually use.

MEN-TAL does not deliberately transmit your mood selections or journal text to these services.

Cookies, local storage, and analytics

The web app does not set advertising or analytics cookies and currently has no analytics or ad technology. It uses browser local storage for the wellbeing features described above; this is device storage, not a cookie. Country lookup is a user-initiated network request, not background tracking. Your website host may use its own strictly necessary security or delivery technology; confirm this with your chosen host before launch. This policy should be updated if cookies, analytics, advertising, or other third-party tools are added.

How long information stays

Web entries remain in browser storage until you clear them, clear that site's browser data, or remove the browser profile. Mobile entries remain on the device until cleared by a feature that removes them, device/app storage is cleared, or the app/device is removed. iOS Keychain data can persist after uninstall. MEN-TAL does not maintain a server-side copy of these entries. A third-party provider's retention is governed by that provider.

Children and sensitive information

MEN-TAL is a general wellbeing resource and is not designed to collect health records or provide clinical care. The operator does not intentionally ask for or collect journal text or mood information on a server. Please do not use MEN-TAL to store details that require professional confidentiality or urgent intervention. A parent or guardian who has questions about a child's use should contact the operator using the privacy contact added above.

Your choices and privacy requests

  • Use MEN-TAL without signing in or sharing journal content.
  • Skip country detection and visit the helpline directory directly.
  • Clear your journal in the app, or remove this site's local storage in your browser settings. You can clear all app data through your device/browser storage controls.
  • Contact the operator to ask questions or make a privacy request. A working contact must be added above before public release; rights and response obligations depend on where you live.

Security and changes

On-device storage reduces the information MEN-TAL receives, but no device or online service can be promised completely secure. Keep your operating system and browser updated and use a device lock. This policy may change when the app, its host, or its external services change. The effective date at the top will be updated when material changes are made.

Important care boundary

MEN-TAL offers general wellbeing ideas, not diagnosis, medical advice, treatment, or crisis support. If you are in immediate danger, call your local emergency number or go to the nearest emergency department. To find emotional support, visit Find A Helpline.
